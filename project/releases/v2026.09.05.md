## Changed decisions

A successful handoff now unambiguously requires acceptance or authorized
reassignment; rejection and escalation finish an attempt, not the transfer or
remaining work. The current owner retains responsibility and records a next
review time or trigger. A missing required specialist, authority, control or
verification cannot be waived by labeling it an accepted risk. Hold dependent
use or seek an authorized qualified alternate; unaffected authorized work can
continue. A low-risk owner-led routine need not acquire a second reviewer
unless policy or risk requires independence.

These are substantive clarifications, potentially incompatible with procedures
that previously treated failed transfer as completion or waived required review.
Review those procedures before adopting. The charter, six concerns, eight SOP
areas, six maintenance activities and technology independence remain intact.

## Edition and Commons migration

New editions use the actual UTC date as YYYY.MM.DD, with numeric .1, .2 suffixes
for additional same-day releases. Chronological date and numeric suffix determine
order; the identifier is not a semantic-version compatibility promise. Never
relabel or add aliases to prior editions. Historical citations and adoption pins
remain valid. No runtime/package/schema version is changed.

[ADR-013](../../decisions/0013-calendar-editions-and-commons-adoption.md) records
Commons provenance, independent authority and adoption without deviations or
deferred shared principles. It accepts human boundaries, bounded conflicts and
honest evidence; Commons tooling and product-specific workflows stay local.

AI Dev Days independently owns teaching updates. Its inspected guide remains
pinned to AI-Native v1.0.0 and Commons v1.0.0. A future teaching revision should
exercise accepted versus attempted handoff, missing mandatory review and a
minimal owner-led routine. No companion release is required or claimed.
