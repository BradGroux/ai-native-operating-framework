# Research Notes

Store source-grounded research that may inform the business framework: existing
operating standards and methods, documented business practices, governance
models, people and AI participating in work, observed problems, and evidence.

Separate source facts from analysis and recommendations. Record what is relevant
to the framework, what should or should not be reused, and what questions remain.

Research may examine technology as context for business work, but technology
implementation does not become part of the framework through a research note.
