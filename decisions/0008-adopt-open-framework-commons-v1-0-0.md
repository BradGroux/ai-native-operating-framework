# ADR-008 — Adopt Open Framework Commons v1.0.0

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-03
- **Owner:** Brad Groux

## Question

Should the AI-Native Operating Framework adopt Open Framework Commons
`v1.0.0`, and if so, what authority, interpretation, and release consequences
follow?

## Decision

The AI-Native Operating Framework adopts
[Open Framework Commons](https://github.com/BradGroux/open-framework-commons)
[`v1.0.0`](https://github.com/BradGroux/open-framework-commons/tree/v1.0.0),
release commit
[a0f0d384e9010a65d1a21a324b4c912<wbr>433d5e031](https://github.com/BradGroux/open-framework-commons/commit/a0f0d384e901),
as shared ecosystem context.

All nine Commons principles are adopted. None are deferred and none require an
explicit deviation. Within this repository, the local authority chain controls.
Commons cannot amend the charter, accepted decisions, canonical framework,
governance, examples, research, roadmap, releases, or implementation choices.
Later Commons revisions require a separate adoption decision.

The people-first principle preserves human purpose, judgment, and
accountability without narrowing the approved AI-native meaning: people and AI
may both perform work under the same business standards and SOPs, with explicit
accountable human ownership. Contribute before extracting is an ecosystem value,
not an additional contribution prerequisite, commercial restriction, business
concern, SOP content requirement, or maintenance activity.

The comparison found no material conflict with Commons `v1.0.0`; none is
accepted or unresolved.

## Consequences

- Readers receive a durable Commons reference in repository orientation and
  Governance.
- The six concerns, eight SOP content areas, shared operating memory standard,
  six maintenance activities, terminology, examples, research, contribution
  process, and governance remain product-local.
- Commons supplies no software, schema, protocol, runtime, technical
  conformance, certification, shared repository machinery, or implementation
  requirement.
- Commons documents are referenced rather than copied into this repository.
- A future apparent conflict remains visible until the responsible authority
  adopts, defers, or deviates from the relevant Commons revision.

## Release Treatment

No downstream adoption or organizational use of the framework is recorded.
The founding steward therefore approves this documentation-only change as a
refresh of the existing `v1.0.0` release rather than a new semantic version.
The original effective date remains 2026-07-30; the refresh is dated
2026-08-03. Commons `v1.0.0` previously resolved to
`27870fb1d57d951b9ef5a3a86f33ef0`<wbr>`68ee557da` and now resolves to
`a0f0d384e9010a65d1a21a324b4c912`<wbr>`433d5e031` after its coordinated
documentation republication. The refreshed Commons diagrams and ecosystem
status do not change this framework's method, local authority, or release
ownership.

This repository's `v1.0.0` will be republished only after this change is
reviewed and merged. Publication then requires final validation, exact
merged-tree verification, and lease-protected replacement of the annotated
tag. The previous product tag object and peeled commit, plus the new merged
release commit, remain recorded in the delivery evidence.

Before replacement, the product's annotated `v1.0.0` tag object was
`3424738c1c3cfdcb1e009789f84a8a33`<wbr>`a1ae0bdb` and its peeled commit was
`2e402d89598849f37e12f6e54c9d7f24`<wbr>`ac5ca76c`.

## Material Dissent and Limitations

No material dissent is recorded. Adoption records philosophical alignment; it
does not establish field validation, certification, compatibility, legal or
professional review, or real-world effectiveness of Commons or this framework.
