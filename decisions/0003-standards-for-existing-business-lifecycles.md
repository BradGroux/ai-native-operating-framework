# ADR-003 — Standards for existing business lifecycles

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-30
- **Owner:** Brad Groux

## Question

Should the framework prescribe one universal operating lifecycle, or define
standards that organizations apply to their existing business lifecycles and
processes?

## Decision

The framework defines business standards that apply to organizations' existing
lifecycles and processes. It identifies what business operations must make clear
and reliable without prescribing one universal sequence for all work.

## Consequences

- Organizations can retain sound existing management systems and process
  lifecycles.
- The framework can apply across materially different business domains.
- Framework requirements will concern the completeness and quality of business
  operations rather than a mandatory sequence of steps.
- Examples may use different lifecycles while demonstrating the same framework
  standards.

## Alternatives considered

A universal lifecycle would be easier to diagram and teach, but it would
duplicate established business methods and force unlike processes into an
artificial sequence.
