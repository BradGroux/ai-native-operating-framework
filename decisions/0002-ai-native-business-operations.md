# ADR-002 — AI-native business operations

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-30
- **Owner:** Brad Groux

## Question

What distinguishes the AI-Native Operating Framework from a conventional
business operating framework?

## Decision

AI-native means the operating framework assumes people and AI may both perform
business work under the same standards and SOPs. It does not require AI
participation in every process or activity, and human accountability remains
explicit.

## Consequences

- The framework governs business work rather than defining separate operating
  rules for people and AI.
- Standards and SOPs must make responsibility, authority, controls, and
  accountability clear enough for the work being performed.
- Organizations can apply the framework to processes with extensive, limited,
  or no current AI participation.
- AI participation does not remove accountable human ownership of business
  outcomes.

## Alternatives considered

Requiring every process to be redesigned around AI participation was rejected.
That approach would make technology adoption the organizing principle rather
than sound business operations.
