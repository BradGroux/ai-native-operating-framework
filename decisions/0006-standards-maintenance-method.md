# ADR-006 — Standards maintenance method

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-30
- **Owner:** Brad Groux

## Question

What practical method should organizations use to create, approve, operate,
review, and improve their business standards and SOPs?

## Decision

Organizations create and maintain standards and SOPs through six practical
activities:

1. **Understand** — observe the actual work and gather governing requirements,
   evidence, and practitioner knowledge.
2. **Document** — describe the standard or SOP using the framework concerns and
   content requirements.
3. **Validate** — walk through normal work, decisions, handoffs, exceptions, and
   failure scenarios with the people responsible for the process.
4. **Approve** — obtain approval from the accountable business owner and any
   required policy or control authorities.
5. **Use** — make the approved standard available, communicate changes, and
   perform the work according to it.
6. **Improve** — review outcomes, evidence, exceptions, incidents, and changed
   requirements; revise and reapprove when warranted.

This is the method for maintaining operating standards. It does not prescribe
the lifecycle of the business processes those standards describe.

## Consequences

- The framework provides a usable method in addition to content standards.
- Organizations may integrate these activities into their existing governance
  and management practices.
- Validation must include normal work and meaningful exception or failure
  scenarios.
- Changed standards return through appropriate validation and approval rather
  than being silently overwritten.

## Alternatives considered

Providing only the content standard was rejected because it would leave the
framework without a practical method for creating and maintaining trustworthy
standards and SOPs.
