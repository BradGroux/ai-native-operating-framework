# ADR-005 — SOP content standard

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-30
- **Owner:** Brad Groux

## Question

What minimum content must an SOP cover under the framework, and should the
standard require that content without imposing one document layout?

## Decision

Every SOP must make the following clear:

1. Purpose, scope, and expected outcome.
2. Accountable owner, participants, responsibilities, and decision authority.
3. Trigger, prerequisites, inputs, and authoritative sources.
4. Activities, decision points, dependencies, handoffs, and outputs.
5. Applicable policies, controls, approvals, and risks.
6. Exceptions, escalation, recovery, and stop conditions.
7. Completion criteria, verification, and required evidence.
8. Review ownership, review cadence or trigger, and change history.

Organizations may combine, rename, or arrange sections as appropriate. The
standard evaluates whether the business meaning is present and clear, not
whether a document follows one template.

## Consequences

- The framework can assess SOP completeness without requiring uniform document
  formatting.
- Existing organizational SOP formats can remain in use when they satisfy the
  content standard.
- Templates may be offered as conveniences or examples but are not the
  framework requirement.
- Cosmetic template compliance cannot substitute for clear and complete
  business meaning.

## Alternatives considered

A single canonical SOP template was rejected because it would fit some domains
poorly and encourage superficial consistency over operating quality.
